Signal generator

Demo only: what the simulated board measures. Like the real ADC it samples the ideal signal with no anti-alias filter, so a tone above half the sample rate folds back, and so do the harmonics of a square wave.

PWM generator

On the board, pin GPIO20 (D0 of header J2): wire it to 43 (through an RC low-pass to read the average) and read it back below. 0.2 Hz to 1 MHz; changing a value restarts the wave.
